# Co_jemy

Personalized recipe proposer. You rate meals 👍/👎; it learns and answers "what should I cook tonight?" with at most four suggestions instead of a wall of search results.

Recipes are retrieved from the [Spoonacular Food API](https://spoonacular.com/food-api) and always link out to the original publisher — the app hosts no recipe content of its own.

Astro 6 (SSR) + React 19 + Tailwind 4 + Supabase, deployed to Cloudflare Workers.
